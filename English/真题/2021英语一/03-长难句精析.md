# 2021 英语（一）· 长难句精析

> 共 62 句。每句按“主干—结构—关键词—翻译—提醒”拆解。建议先遮住解析，自己说一遍主干。

## S01　完形　难度 ●●○

**原句：** Fluid intelligence is the type of intelligence that has to do with short-term memory and the ability to think quickly, logically, and abstractly in order to solve new problems.

**主干：** Fluid intelligence is the type of intelligence.

**结构：** that has to do with... 是定语从句修饰 intelligence；to think... 修饰 ability；in order to solve... 是目的状语。

**关键词：** have to do with 与……有关；abstractly 抽象地；in order to 为了。

**参考译文：** 流体智力是一种与短时记忆以及快速、逻辑、抽象地思考以解决新问题的能力有关的智力。

**提醒：** 先圈出两个并列宾语：short-term memory 和 the ability；不要把 to think 误当成谓语。

## S02　完形　难度 ●○○

**原句：** It peaks in young adulthood, levels out for a period of time, and then generally starts to slowly decline as we age.

**主干：** It peaks, levels out, and then starts to decline.

**结构：** 三个并列谓语；in young adulthood / for a period of time / as we age 都是时间状语。

**关键词：** peak 达到顶峰；level out 趋于平稳；decline 下降；as 随着。

**参考译文：** 它在成年早期达到顶峰，随后在一段时间内保持平稳，然后通常随着年龄增长开始缓慢下降。

**提醒：** as we age 是“随着我们变老”，不是“当我们变老时”那一次动作。

## S03　完形　难度 ●●●

**原句：** But while aging is inevitable, scientists are finding out that certain changes in brain function may not be.

**主干：** Scientists are finding out that certain changes may not be.

**结构：** while 引导让步状语从句；that 引导宾语从句；may not be 后省略了 inevitable。

**关键词：** inevitable 不可避免的；may not be 后省略表语。

**参考译文：** 但是，尽管衰老不可避免，科学家们正发现，大脑功能的某些变化也许并非不可避免。

**提醒：** 英语常用省略避免重复；把 be 后面的 inevitable 补回来，逻辑就通了。

## S04　完形　难度 ●●○

**原句：** One study found that muscle loss and the accumulation of body fat around the abdomen are associated with a decline in fluid intelligence.

**主干：** One study found that A and B are associated with C.

**结构：** that 引导宾语从句；从句主语是 muscle loss and the accumulation...，谓语用 are；around the abdomen 修饰 fat。

**关键词：** accumulation 积累；be associated with 与……相关；abdomen 腹部。

**参考译文：** 一项研究发现，肌肉流失和腹部脂肪的堆积与流体智力下降有关。

**提醒：** A and B 作主语时谓语用复数 are；不要被中间的 of body fat 干扰。

## S05　完形　难度 ●●●

**原句：** The researchers looked at data that included measurements of lean muscle and abdominal fat from more than 4,000 middle-to-older-aged men and women and compared that data to reported changes in fluid intelligence over a six-year period.

**主干：** The researchers looked at data and compared that data to changes.

**结构：** that included... 是定语从句；from... 修饰 measurements；and compared 与 looked at 并列；over a six-year period 是时间状语。

**关键词：** measurement 测量值；lean muscle 瘦肌肉；compare A to B 把 A 与 B 比较。

**参考译文：** 研究人员查看了包含 4,000 多名中老年男女瘦肌肉和腹部脂肪测量数据的数据集，并将这些数据与六年期间报告的流体智力变化进行了比较。

**提醒：** 句子的两个大动词是 looked 和 compared，中间的 that included 只是修饰 data。

## S06　完形　难度 ●●○

**原句：** They found that middle-aged people with higher measures of abdominal fat scored worse on measures of fluid intelligence as the years went by.

**主干：** They found that middle-aged people scored worse.

**结构：** that 引导宾语从句；with higher measures... 是介词短语作后置定语；as the years went by 是时间状语从句。

**关键词：** score 得分；go by（时间）流逝；measure 指标。

**参考译文：** 他们发现，腹部脂肪指标较高的中年人在岁月流逝中，流体智力测试的表现更差。

**提醒：** with 短语修饰 people，不是谓语；主句谓语是 scored。

## S07　Text 1　难度 ●●○

**原句：** It has become a grimly reliable annual ritual: every January the cost of travelling by train rises, imposing a significant extra burden on those who have no option but to use the rail network to get to work or otherwise.

**主干：** It has become a ritual.

**结构：** 冒号后解释 ritual；imposing... 是现在分词作结果状语；who have no option but to... 是定语从句。

**关键词：** grimly 冷酷地；ritual 惯例；impose...on... 把……强加于；have no option but to 别无选择只能。

**参考译文：** 这已经成了一种冷酷而可靠的年度惯例：每年 1 月，乘火车出行的成本都会上涨，给那些别无选择、只能依靠铁路网络上班或出行的人增加了一笔不小的负担。

**提醒：** 冒号不是句号，后面仍在解释前面的 ritual；imposing 的逻辑主语是“涨价”这件事。

## S08　Text 1　难度 ●●○

**原句：** This year's rise, an average of 2.7 per cent, may be a fraction lower than last year's, but it is still well above the official Consumer Price Index (CPI) measure of inflation.

**主干：** This year's rise may be lower, but it is above the CPI measure.

**结构：** an average of 2.7 per cent 是插入语；may be 表推测；but 连接转折分句；well above 表示“远高于”。

**关键词：** a fraction 一点点；well above 远高于；inflation 通货膨胀。

**参考译文：** 今年的涨幅平均为 2.7%，或许比去年略低一点，但仍远高于官方通胀指标——消费者价格指数（CPI）。

**提醒：** 先把插入语括起来，主干就是“涨幅可能低，但仍高于 CPI”。

## S09　Text 1　难度 ●●●

**原句：** Successive governments have permitted such increases on the grounds that the cost of investing in and running the rail network should be borne by those who use it, rather than the general taxpayer.

**主干：** Governments have permitted increases.

**结构：** on the grounds that... 引导原因同位语从句；should be borne 是被动；who use it 是定语从句；rather than 表对比。

**关键词：** successive 历届的；on the grounds that 以……为理由；bear 承担；taxpayer 纳税人。

**参考译文：** 历届政府都允许这种涨价，理由是投资和运营铁路网络的成本应由使用者承担，而不是由普通纳税人承担。

**提醒：** bear 的过去分词是 borne；be borne by 是“由……承担”。

## S10　Text 1　难度 ●●○

**主干：** Why should a pensioner subsidise the commute?

**结构：** the argument goes 是插入语；why 引导修辞疑问句；from... 是后置定语。

## S12　Text 1　难度 ●●○

**主干：** There is a strong case that...

**关键词：** a strong case 有充分理由；industrial action 罢工行动；compensation 赔偿；disruption 中断。

## S14　Text 1　难度 ●●●

**原句：** Yes, more investment is needed, but passengers will not be willing to pay more indefinitely if they must also endure cramped, unreliable services, punctuated by regular chaos when timetables are changed, or planned maintenance is managed incompetently.

**参考译文：** 在西欧许多国家，20 世纪 60 年代，高等教育的学生人数在五年内翻了一番；到 20 世纪 70 年代中期，又在七年、八年或十年内再次翻番。

**提醒：** doubled again 要译出“再次翻番”，不能只译“又翻倍”而不交代时间。

## S56　翻译 49　难度 ●●●

**原句：** And when the new staff are predominantly young men and women fresh from postgraduate study, they largely define the norms of academic life in that faculty.

**主干：** They define the norms.

**结构：** when 引导时间状语从句；fresh from... 是后置定语；in that faculty 是地点状语。

**关键词：** predominantly 主要地；fresh from 刚从……毕业；norm 规范；faculty 院系。

**参考译文：** 而当新员工主要是刚完成研究生学业的年轻男女时，他们在很大程度上界定了该院系学术生活的规范。

**提醒：** faculty 在这里是“院系”，不是“全体教员”或“能力”。

## S57　翻译 50　难度 ●●●

**原句：** High growth rates increased the chances for academic innovation; they also weakened the forms and processes by which teachers and students are admitted into a community of scholars during periods of stability or slow growth.

**主干：** High growth rates increased the chances; they also weakened the forms and processes.

**结构：** 分号连接两个并列分句；by which... 是定语从句；are admitted into 是被动。

**关键词：** chances for 机会；weaken 削弱；be admitted into 被接纳进入；community of scholars 学者共同体。

**参考译文：** 高增长率增加了学术创新的机会；但它们也削弱了在稳定或缓慢增长时期教师和学生被接纳为学者共同体的形式和程序。

**提醒：** by which 不能漏译，它说明“通过哪些形式和程序”被接纳。

## S58　翻译 · 扩展　难度 ●●●

**原句：** But after the war, great social and political changes arising out of the successful war against Fascism created a growing demand in European and American economies for increasing numbers of graduates with more than a secondary school education.

**主干：** Changes created a demand.

**结构：** arising out of... 是现在分词定语；in European and American economies 是地点状语；for increasing numbers... 说明 demand。

**关键词：** arise out of 由……产生；Fascism 法西斯主义；secondary school education 中学教育。

**参考译文：** 但战后，战胜法西斯主义所带来的巨大社会和政治变革，使欧美经济体对越来越多受过中学以上教育的毕业生产生了日益增长的需求。

**提醒：** demand for... 被地点状语隔开，先找到 demand 和 for 的搭配。

## S59　翻译 · 扩展　难度 ●●○

**原句：** These demands resulted in a very rapid expansion of the systems of higher education, beginning in the 1960s and developing very rapidly (though unevenly) during the 1970s and 1980s.

**主干：** These demands resulted in expansion.

**结构：** beginning 和 developing 是现在分词补充说明；though unevenly 是插入语。

**关键词：** result in 导致；expansion 扩张；unevenly 不平衡地。

**参考译文：** 这些需求导致高等教育体系迅速扩张，从 20 世纪 60 年代开始，并在 70 年代和 80 年代非常迅速地发展（尽管并不均衡）。

**提醒：** beginning 的逻辑主语是 expansion，不是 demands。

## S60　翻译 · 扩展　难度 ●●○

**原句：** The growth of higher education manifests itself in at least three quite different ways, and these in turn have given rise to different sets of problems.

**主干：** The growth manifests itself, and these have given rise to problems.

**结构：** and 连接并列分句；in turn 表示“反过来”。

**关键词：** manifest itself 体现；give rise to 引起；in turn 反过来。

**参考译文：** 高等教育的增长至少以三种相当不同的方式表现出来，而这些方式又反过来引发了不同的问题。

**提醒：** these 指代 three ways；in turn 不是“依次”。

## S61　翻译 · 扩展　难度 ●●○

**原句：** Each of these manifestations of growth carried its own peculiar problems in its wake.

**主干：** Each carried problems.

**结构：** of growth 修饰 manifestations；in its wake 是状语。

**关键词：** manifestation 表现；peculiar 特有的；in its wake 随之而来。

**参考译文：** 这些增长表现中的每一种，都随之带来了其特有的问题。

**提醒：** in its wake 是“随之而来”，不是“在它醒来时”。

## S62　翻译 · 扩展　难度 ●●●

**原句：** And if the postgraduate student population also grows rapidly and there is loss of a close apprenticeship relationship between faculty members and students, the student culture becomes the chief socializing force for new postgraduate students, with consequences for the intellectual and academic life of the institution.

**主干：** The student culture becomes the chief socializing force.

**结构：** if 引导条件从句，包含两个并列条件；with consequences for... 是结果状语。

**关键词：** apprenticeship 师徒关系；socializing force 社会化力量；consequence 后果。

**参考译文：** 如果研究生人数也迅速增长，教师与学生之间紧密的师徒关系随之消失，那么学生文化就会成为新研究生的主要社会化力量，并对该机构的知识和学术生活产生影响。

**提醒：** there is loss of... 是第二个条件，不是主句；主句在逗号后。
